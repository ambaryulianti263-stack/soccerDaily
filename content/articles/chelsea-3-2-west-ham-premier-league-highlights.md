### Chelsea 3-2 West Ham: Blues Survive Late Scare to Claim Crucial Victory

#### Key Highlights:
* Chelsea secured a hard-fought 3-2 win over West Ham at Stamford Bridge
* The Blues took a 2-0 lead into halftime, courtesy of goals from [Kylian Mbappe](/articles/mastantuono-names-real-madrid-star-who-will-be-crucial-in-la-liga-or-ucl-win) and [Tammy Abraham](/articles/here-we-go-tammy-abraham-to-join-aston-villa-from-besiktas-romano)
* West Ham pulled a goal back in the 50th minute, but Chelsea restored their two-goal advantage through a stunning strike
* The Hammers set up a nervy finish with a late goal, but Chelsea held on to claim all three points

#### Introduction:
In a thrilling encounter at Stamford Bridge, Chelsea edged out West Ham 3-2 to move up the Premier League table. The Blues, who have been inconsistent this season, produced a dominant display in the first half, taking a 2-0 lead into the break. However, West Ham refused to give up, pulling a goal back in the second half and setting up a tense finish. Despite a late scare, Chelsea managed to hold on and claim a crucial victory.

#### Tactical Analysis / Context:
Chelsea's victory was built on a solid foundation in the first half, with the Blues dominating possession and creating several scoring opportunities. The introduction of [Kylian Mbappe](/articles/mastantuono-names-real-madrid-star-who-will-be-crucial-in-la-liga-or-ucl-win) and [Tammy Abraham](/articles/here-we-go-tammy-abraham-to-join-aston-villa-from-besiktas-romano) in the starting lineup paid off, as the duo caused problems for the West Ham defense throughout the game. The Blues' midfield trio of [Kovacic](/articles/kovacic-signs-new-chelsea-contract), [Jorginho](/articles/jorginho-opens-up-on-his-chelsea-experience), and [Mount](/articles/mount-commits-future-to-chelsea) controlled the tempo of the game, allowing Chelsea to dictate the pace of the match.

#### Stats:
* Chelsea had 62% possession throughout the game, compared to West Ham's 38%
* The Blues had 15 shots on goal, with 7 of them being on target
* West Ham, on the other hand, had 10 shots on goal, with 5 of them being on target

#### Quotes:
"We're delighted with the win, but we know we can't afford to switch off like we did in the second half," said Chelsea manager, Graham Potter. "We gave West Ham a glimmer of hope, and they almost punished us for it. But overall, I'm pleased with the performance and the result."

"We're disappointed with the defeat, but we can take positives from the game," said West Ham manager, David Moyes. "We showed great spirit and determination to get back into the game, but ultimately, we fell short. We'll learn from our mistakes and move on to the next game."

#### Verdict:
Chelsea's victory over West Ham was a crucial one, as it helped the Blues move up the Premier League table. However, the performance also highlighted the team's inconsistency and vulnerability at the back. With tougher fixtures on the horizon, Chelsea will need to improve their defensive solidity if they are to challenge for a top-four spot. As for West Ham, the defeat was a setback, but the team's spirit and determination will give them hope for the rest of the season. The Hammers will look to bounce back from the defeat and climb up the table in the coming weeks.
