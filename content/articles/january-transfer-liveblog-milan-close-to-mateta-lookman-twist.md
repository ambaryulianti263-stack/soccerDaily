The January transfer window is in full swing, and the rumor mill is churning out some exciting news. ### Tactical Analysis / Context
Milan's pursuit of Mateta is a clear indication of their desire to strengthen their attacking lineup. On the other hand, Lookman's move to Nottingham Forest has been thrown into doubt due to a disagreement over the transfer fee. Forest's failure to sign Lookman could be a significant blow to their hopes of avoiding relegation.
