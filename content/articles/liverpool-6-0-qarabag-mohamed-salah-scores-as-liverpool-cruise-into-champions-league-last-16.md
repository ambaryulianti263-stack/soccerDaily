### Introduction
In a thrilling display of football, Liverpool cruised into the Champions League last 16 with a resounding 6-0 victory over Qarabag. The match, played at Anfield, saw the Reds dominate from start to finish, with Mohamed Salah scoring one of the goals. The win marks Liverpool's fifth consecutive victory in the Champions League, and they finish top of their group with an impressive performance. But what made this match so special, and how did Liverpool manage to thrash their opponents so convincingly?

From the kickoff, it was clear that Liverpool were the superior team. They dominated possession, with their midfield trio of Jordan Henderson, Thiago Alcantara, and Fabinho controlling the tempo of the game. The Reds' attack, led by Salah, Roberto Firmino, and Sadio Mane, was relentless, with wave after wave of attacks testing the Qarabag defense. The Azerbaijani team, on the other hand, struggled to cope with Liverpool's intensity, and their defense was breached time and time again. As the match wore on, Liverpool's dominance only grew, with the scoreline reflecting their superiority.

The stats from the match make for impressive reading. Liverpool had 24 shots on goal, with 12 of those on target. Qarabag, on the other hand, managed just two shots on goal, with neither of those troubling Liverpool goalkeeper Alisson. These stats demonstrate Liverpool's control and dominance over the match, and highlight the gulf in class between the two teams.
