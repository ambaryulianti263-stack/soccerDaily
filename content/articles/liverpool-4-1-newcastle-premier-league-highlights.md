### Key Highlights
* Liverpool defeat Newcastle 4-1 at Anfield
* Mohamed Salah scores twice in the dominant display
* Newcastle's poor run of form continues with another loss
* Liverpool move up the Premier League table with the win

### Introduction
In an electrifying display of football, Liverpool thrashed Newcastle 4-1 at Anfield, showcasing their incredible skill and teamwork. The Reds, led by the unstoppable Mohamed Salah, dominated the Magpies from start to finish, leaving the visitors reeling. This victory marks a significant milestone for Liverpool, as they continue to climb the Premier League table. But what made this match so special, and how will it impact the teams' future performances?

### Tactical Analysis / Context
Liverpool's manager, Jurgen Klopp, opted for a 4-3-3 formation, with Salah, Roberto Firmino, and Luis Diaz forming a deadly attacking trio. The home team's high-intensity pressing and clever movement off the ball allowed them to control the game, creating numerous scoring opportunities. Newcastle, on the other hand, struggled to cope with Liverpool's pace and precision, often finding themselves on the back foot. As the match progressed, the Magpies' defense became increasingly fragile, ultimately leading to their downfall.

### Stats
* Liverpool had 23 shots on goal, with 8 of them being on target
* Newcastle managed only 4 shots on goal, with 1 being on target
* The Reds enjoyed 62% possession, while the Magpies had 38%
* Mohamed Salah scored his 10th and 11th Premier League goals of the season

### Quotes
"We were exceptional today, and I'm delighted with the performance," said Jurgen Klopp in a post-match interview. "The lads worked tirelessly, and our attacking play was top-notch. We're getting closer to where we want to be, and this win is a huge confidence booster."

Newcastle manager Eddie Howe commented, "We were outplayed and outclassed. Liverpool were too strong, too fast, and too clinical. We need to regroup and reassess our strategy, as we can't afford to concede goals like that."

### Verdict
With this convincing win, Liverpool have moved up the Premier League table, while Newcastle's struggles continue. The Magpies will need to regroup and find a way to stop their leaky defense, or risk being dragged into a relegation battle. As for Liverpool, they'll look to build on this momentum, pushing for a top-four finish. The Reds' next match against [Chelsea](/articles/chelsea-vs-west-ham) will be a crucial test of their mettle, while Newcastle face a tough trip to [Everton](/articles/everton-vs-leeds-united). The Premier League is heating up, and these teams will need to be at their best to achieve their goals.
